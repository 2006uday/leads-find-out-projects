About the hotel

Simple, honest hospitality in South Delhi

Hotel Ambica Palace is a 3-star hotel in Safdarjung Enclave. It is built around what visitors to this part of Delhi actually need: a clean air-conditioned room, a private bathroom, reliable Wi-Fi, and a front desk that is awake whenever you arrive.

Many of our guests are travelling for medical care at AIIMS or Safdarjung Hospital, or accompanying a family member. Others are here for business meetings, shopping at Sarojini Nagar, or a weekend in the city. The hotel accepts local ID and welcomes couples and families.
