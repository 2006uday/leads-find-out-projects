Amenities, policies & guest services

What the hotel provides, how check-in works, which ID we accept and the simple rules that keep every stay comfortable.

Reception

A warm welcome, any hour

Our carved-wood reception sits in a bright, marble-floored lobby with comfortable seating. The desk is staffed 24 hours, so you can check in late, ask for directions to the hospital, or arrange a cab at any time.

Guests are greeted at the desk with a bowl of rose petals, a small touch that sets the tone for the stay.

Guest services

Everything for a stress-free stay

Our team is on hand around the clock to help with arrivals, directions, cabs and local needs.

Policies

Clear rules, no surprises

ID & check-in

  • Local ID is accepted at this property.
  • Every adult guest should carry a valid photo ID.
  • Couple-friendly stays; ID verification applies to all guests.
  • Standard check-in is from 2 PM; please confirm at booking.

Stay rules

  • Pets are not allowed.
  • Please keep noise low in the evening for fellow guests.
  • Check-out is by 12 noon; ask the front desk about late check-out.
  • Smoking and cancellation terms follow the listing’s house rules.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do you accept local ID?

Yes. Local ID is accepted. Please still carry a valid photo ID for every adult guest.

Is the front desk open at night?

Yes, the front desk operates 24 hours, so late or early arrivals are fine.

Is parking available?

Free parking is available for guests, subject to space on the day.

Can I bring a pet?

No, pets are not allowed at the property.

Is breakfast available?

An on-site restaurant is available. Breakfast inclusion depends on the rate you book, so check it on the listing.

Policies can change. The Booking.com listing carries the latest house rules.
