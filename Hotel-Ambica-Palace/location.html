    How to spot us

    Look for the lit-up sign

    Hotel Ambica Palace stands at A-1/300 in Block A-1, Safdarjung Enclave. The illuminated “Ambica Palace” sign sits above a glass-fronted reception with a small green-matted entrance step, easy to spot even late at night.

    Parking is available at the front, and the reception is staffed around the clock if you need help finding us.

    Getting here

    Easy to reach from across Delhi

    The hotel is about 14 km from Indira Gandhi International Airport, so a taxi or cab ride typically makes the most sense after a flight. Delhi Metro stations serving AIIMS and the Safdarjung area are nearby, and auto-rickshaws and app cabs are easy to find locally. Ask the front desk for directions or help arranging a pickup.

    Visiting a patient

    Safdarjung Hospital is about an 8-minute walk and AIIMS about 15, so you can come and go between visiting hours without a cab.

    Business & errands

    Sarojini Nagar Market, Dilli Haat and Hauz Khas are close for shopping, meals and meetings.

    Sightseeing

    Lodhi Gardens and Gandhi Smriti are a short ride away, with Safdarjung’s Tomb and Hauz Khas Fort nearby.
